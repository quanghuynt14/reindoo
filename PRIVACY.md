# Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This policy covers Reindoo for iOS and Reindoo Hán, its Chinese–Vietnamese edition. Both work the same way.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Reindoo for iOS has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network request.

## What stays on your phone

The dictionary ships inside the app. The words you look up, and your progress in reels, are stored on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Links

An entry can link to its page on Wiktionary. Tapping the link opens your browser. From there, the [Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) applies.

## Speech

The app reads words aloud with the speech voices built into iOS. The audio is produced on your phone.

## Contact

Questions go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/quanghuynt14/reindoo/issues).
