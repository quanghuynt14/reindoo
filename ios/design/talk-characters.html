Hươu và Chó rừng

Full bodies now: feelings show in the arms and the head as much as in the face. The wolf became a chacal, the word French Nonviolent Communication uses. Same three dry tools, warmer outlines, one quiet colour behind each animal.

La Girafe Hươu cao cổ

Listening: the head tilts and a hoof rests on her heart. Happy: both arms up, and she hops.

Le Chacal Chó rừng

Grumpy, never scary. Cross: arms folded. Sulky: head down, ears flat. Softened: he holds out a paw.

Ensemble Cùng nhau

The last card of a story: the jackal has calmed down, and the giraffe keeps him company.

  • Sepia and plum pencilWarm outlines, drawn twice, slightly apart. Lines hidden behind a part are left out, as a hand would.
  • Coloured pencilFills hatched right-handed, over the edges, with the paper showing through. Shadows in violet, not grey.
  • Soft pastelOne flat colour behind each animal, a shadow on the ground, cheeks, and a little light.

On a card

The heads at button size. Tap Chó rừng or Hươu to answer: the giraffe hops when you're right, and the jackal shakes his head when you're not.

Every stroke comes from a fixed seed, so the drawing is the same on every phone. In the app, the same rules would run in Swift.
